# CATCH NOTHING: THE USELESSNESS ENGINE

> *"Turning Effort Into Absolutely Nothing Since Never."*  
> An intentionally futile, absurd, chaotic, and entertaining browser game.

Pure HTML5 + CSS3 + vanilla JavaScript. No external dependencies, no backend, works 100% offline.

---

## 🚀 How to Run

1. Open `catch-nothing/index.html` directly in any modern browser (Chrome, Edge, Firefox, Safari).
2. Or open via local static server / Live Server.
3. Select difficulty: **Easy Nothing**, **Medium Nothing**, or **Impossible Nothing**.
4. Click **PLAY GAME** (or whatever sarcastic label the button has right now).
5. Prepare to achieve absolutely nothing.

---

## 🎮 Controls

| Action | Desktop | Mobile / Touch |
|---|---|---|
| **Move cursor** | Move mouse (arena captures mouse pointer) | Drag finger inside arena |
| **Catch** | Click (or `Space`) | Tap the **CATCH** button |
| **Pause / Resume** | `P` or Pause button | Pause button |
| **Release mouse** | `Esc` (auto-pauses the game) | n/a |

## 🌟 Upgraded Features

### 1. Fake Victory System
- When you reach the milestone, a massive celebratory screen triggers with confetti, triumph fanfare, and golden trophies declaring you have achieved nothing.
- After a short delay, a dramatic record scratch hits and a huge red stamp slams: **`ACTUALLY, YOU LOST.`**

### 2. Mouse Betrayal Mode
- Random event where your cursor revolts, rotates, glides, or inverts unexpectedly.
- Announcements: *"YOUR MOUSE HAS RESIGNED."*, *"CONTROL TRANSFERRED TO AN UNPAID INTERN."*
- Brief, fair, and temporary.

### 3. Useless AI Life Coach
- Click **Ask AI Coach** for over 35 absurd, unhelpful existential life tips.
- Predefined offline neural network simulation. Earns +10 NothingCoins per consultation.

### 4. Random Nonsense Event Engine
Triggers chaotic events periodically:
- **Chicken Invasion:** Animated chickens strut across the arena with clucking sounds.
- **Disco Mode:** The arena floor pulses funky colors while the dot dances rhythmically.
- **Raining Dots:** Harmless decorative dots drizzle from the top.
- **Snail Mode:** Temporal dilation slows movement to 35%.
- **Ghost Mode:** The dot phase-shifts into a translucent phantom.
- **Courtroom Drama:** *"THE COURT FINDS YOU GUILTY OF CHASING CIRCLES."*
- **Tax Audit:** Department of Nothingness issues a 15% tax invoice on zero accomplishments.
- **Meteor Shower:** Tiny pixel meteors streak across the screen.
- **Time Confusion:** Countdown timer displays *"TIME IS A SOCIAL CONSTRUCT"*.
- **Dot Strike:** Dot halts movement and displays a strike protest sign.
- **Fake Update:** *"INSTALLING UPDATE: IMPROVED NOTHINGNESS — 99%"*.

### 5. Fake News Ticker
- A continuous breaking news ticker at the top with over 45 satirical headlines reporting on your futility.

### 6. Useless Power-Ups
Clickable orbs that appear with hilarious zero-benefit effects:
- **Future Vision:** Shows where the dot was 3 seconds ago.
- **Magnetic Mouse:** Attracts fake decoys straight to your cursor.
- **Invisible Shield:** Protects you from 0 incoming attacks.
- **Time Machine:** Rewinds time by 0.00 seconds.
- **Double Points:** Adds 500 points and explains they have no value.
- **Golden Cursor:** Makes your cursor shiny without improving your accuracy.
- **Anti-Gravity:** Causes ambient particles to float into the sky.
- **Luck Potion:** *"YOUR LUCK HAS IMPROVED BY 0.000%"*.
- **Emergency Button:** Dramatic siren that resolves into nothing.

### 7. 32 Sarcastic Achievements
- Includes all original achievements (`Beginner's Luck`, `Professional Misclicker`, `Brain.exe Stopped Working`, `Unemployment Simulator`, `Certified Nothing Catcher`, `Professional Idiot`) plus 26 new achievements with rewards and tracking.

### 8. The Department of Nothingness
- Interactive bureaucratic office managing Form 404 permits to pursue circular pixels, rubber-stamp rejections, and fee collections.

### 9. 24/7 Ignored Customer Support
- Chat with **Agent Glitch #404**, choose your complaint, and receive over 28 automated dismissive responses.

### 10. Absurd Statistics Dashboard
- Real metrics (clicks, catches, misses, cursor distance in km) combined with useless calculations (NASA interest: 0%, comparison to a potato: inconclusive).

### 11. Bartholomew the Cat Companion
- An animated cat sits by the arena, naps, purrs, and offers biting commentary. Click the cat to hear it meow and unlock a secret achievement.

### 12. 8 Boss Stages & The Grand Finale
Progression expanded with 8 distinct boss encounters:
1. `THE DOT` (Hyper-evasive)
2. `THE BIGGER DOT` (Colossal 70px entity)
3. `INVISIBLE DOT` (Fades into nothingness)
4. `DOT'S LAWYER` (Cease & Desist summons)
5. `DOT'S MANAGER` (Q3 Performance Review)
6. `DOT'S PARENTS` ("Why can't you be a square?")
7. `DEPT OF NOTHINGNESS` (Bureaucratic red tape shield)
8. `THE FINAL NOTHING` (Cosmic void finale)

### 13. NothingCoins Economy & Shop
- Earn **NothingCoins (NC)** by missing, consulting the AI, surviving nonsense events, and failing.
- Spend coins in **The Nothing Shop** on items like *Golden Nothing*, *Premium Nothing*, *Invisible Trophy*, and *Lifetime Nothing Subscription*.

### 14. Printable Certificate of Absolute Uselessness
- Finish the final boss or access the certificate directly from the Game Over screen.
- Includes official seal, serial number, signatures from the Minister of Pointless Endeavors, and clean browser printing support via **PRINT MY SHAME**.
- Click **SHARE NOTHING** to copy your futile record to the clipboard.
